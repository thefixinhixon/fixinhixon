Cannabis Concentrates Quick Guide

What Are Cannabis Concentrates?

Cannabis concentrates are highly potent products made by extracting cannabinoids and terpenes from cannabis flower. These products typically contain 50–90%+ THC and are much stronger than traditional flower (10–30% THC).

  • Shatter: Brittle, glass-like, very potent
  • Wax/Budder: Soft, creamy, aromatic
  • Live Resin: Saucy, preserves terpenes/flavor
  • Rosin: Sappy, solventless, pure
  • Hash: Powder or pressed, classic method
  • Distillate: Thick oil, ultra-refined, used in vapes and edibles
  • Diamonds/Sauce: Crystalized THCA with terpene “sauce”

Solventless vs. Solvent-Free

Term What It Means Examples
Solventless Made with no chemical solvents ever. Only mechanical methods (heat, pressure, water, sieving). Rosin, Bubble Hash, Dry Sift
Solvent-Free May use solvents in processing, but the final product is fully purged—no solvent residue remains. Distillate, BHO Wax, Shatter
All products sold here are tested and solvent-free by law.

Consumption Methods

  • Dabbing: Dab rig, e-nail, or pen (small amount, high heat)
  • Vaping: Prefilled cartridges (distillate or live resin)
  • Smoking: Hash or rosin can be added to joints or bowls
  • Edibles: Use distillate or hash (must be decarbed)

Safety & Customer Tips

  • Start low, go slow—concentrates are much stronger than flower!
  • Recommend pre-filled carts or hash for beginners; reserve dabbing, shatter, or diamonds for experienced customers.
  • Store most concentrates in a cool, dry place (live resin and rosin do best in the fridge).
  • All products are lab-tested for potency and safety.

Budtender Best Practices

  • Ask: “Have you tried concentrates before?”
  • Guide by experience: suggest easier products for newbies.
  • Explain the difference between solventless and solvent-free.
  • Never claim concentrates are “safer”—they’re more potent and require care!
Quick FAQ
  • What’s best for a first-timer?
    Hash or prefilled vape cartridges—easy to use and dose.
  • Is solventless better?
    It’s a matter of preference; all legal products are safe.
  • How should I store these?
    Cool, dark, and airtight. Fridge for live resin/rosin.
