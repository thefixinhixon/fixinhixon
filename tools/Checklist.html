Dispensary Shift Checklist & Operational Compliance

Store Policy & Accountability Statement (Click to expand)

Operational accuracy, security, and cleanliness are non-negotiable standards at this store. Every employee on duty is individually and collectively responsible for completing the daily task checklist for their shift.

  • Inventory Audit: Spot checks and count reconciliations must be executed daily without exception.
  • Vault Back-Stock Check: Audit vault back-stock every shift and bring floor-ready stock to the sales floor immediately.
  • Store Cleanliness: A dirty store, smeared display glass, cluttered budtender station, or unsanitary restroom is completely unacceptable.

* Failure to complete assigned shift tasks or record initials will result in formal disciplinary action up to and including termination.

Phase 1: OPEN SHIFT TASKS (30–45 Mins Before Doors Open)
  • Safe & Register Setup
    Count and verify register tills; log starting float in POS.
  • Opening Sanitation
    Wipe down front entry glass, check-in counter, and sales floor display cases.
  • ID / Check-In Station Prep
    Ensure ID scanners are powered on, guest log sheets are stocked, and entry area is clear.
  • POS & Station Check
    Verify each budtender terminal has receipt paper, exit bags, inventory labels, etc.
  • Opening Floor Visual Audit
    Ensure display cases are well-lit, price tags face forward, and all displayed products have prices.
Phase 2: MID-DAY SHIFT TASKS
  • Slack Category Cycle Count
    Complete cycle count for category assigned in Slack for the current day.
  • Delivery Intake Prep
    Ensure intake desk/vault receiving area is clear of debris for vendor deliveries.
Phase 3: ANYTIME / ACTIVE FLOOR DUTIES
  • Vault Back-Stock & Floor Restock NON-NEGOTIABLE ANYTIME
    • Audit stock levels in main vault; identify low floor stock.
    • Transfer available back-stock from vault to floor display/drawers and log all transfers in POS.
  • Continuous Cleanliness Sweeps NON-NEGOTIABLE ANYTIME
    • Wipe display glass free of fingerprints after every rush.
    • Empty sales floor trash and recycling bins before reaching 3/4 full.
    • Sweep sales floor and check-in lobby; keep counter areas free of personal clutter.
Phase 4: CLOSING SHIFT & LOCKUP (Post-Store Hours)
  • Sales Floor Restock & Cash Drops
    Replenish floor drawers from vault. Count tills down, make safe drops, and print shift summary. Restock cash drawers from vault.
  • Restroom Deep Clean & Restock NON-NEGOTIABLE
    • Scrub toilet, sink, counter & mirror; sanitize handles/switches.
    • Restock soap, paper towels, and toilet paper; mop floor and empty trash.
  • Floor Deep Clean & Vault Securement
    Disinfect POS terminals, mop sales floor, remove all trash, secure vault, and arm alarm.
