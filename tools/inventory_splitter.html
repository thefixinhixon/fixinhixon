How to use the Inventory Splitter

  • Step 1: Upload your CSV. It must have Product and Remaining Count columns.
  • Step 2: Choose a split ratio (preset buttons, or enter your own for Midtown, Alto, Carrizozo).
  • Step 3: Click "Split Inventory".
  • Step 4: Exclude products (uncheck box in table) or search/filter by product name.
  • Step 5: Download results as CSV or print for your records.

Notes: All splits use whole units only. Any leftover units always go to Midtown.
